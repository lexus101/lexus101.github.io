02 / CHARACTERS

One era. Many lives.

Current focus: characters connected to Elisa's Arvania story.

STUDENT · FIGHTER

Elisa

Brilliant, direct, protective—and slowly learning that strength does not give her the right to choose for everyone else.

HEALER · SUPPORT

Claire

Gentle, observant, and deeply loyal. Her path is learning to stand beside people without disappearing inside them.

FIGHTER · DREAMER

Leo

A natural optimist with a hero's heart—the kind of person who makes others believe they do not have to carry everything alone.

ANALYST · NOBLE

Valri

Reserved, philosophical, and trained to look composed. Under the manners is someone still deciding what he truly believes.

DUELIST · RIVAL

Vivian

Elegant, precise, proud. She admires Elisa almost as much as she resents living in her shadow.

REFORMER · REVOLUTIONARY

Mordia / “Klaus”

A former slave who inherits another man's name, dream, political power—and eventually the unbearable weight of a revolution.
