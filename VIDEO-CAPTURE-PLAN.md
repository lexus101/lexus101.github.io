# Portfolio Capture Plan

Record at 1920×1080 (or native resolution), 60 fps if stable. Keep each clip short, focused, and free of setup/debug UI when possible. Move the mouse deliberately; avoid narration for these loops.

## Conquer
1. **Command Scale — 10–15s**: box-select several armies, issue one coordinated movement, then split or reinforce. Show “large intention, few inputs.”
2. **Route / Exhaustion — 8–12s**: show a formation going from stable → shaking/spreading → routing. Prefer a clear before/after in one shot.
3. **King + War Mist — 10–15s**: reveal/find an enemy king, show a feint or opening, then attempt/complete a decapitation strike. The important part is information changing the decision.
4. **Comeback Replay — 15–25s**: use the saved 200vs1000 replay. Capture the disadvantaged state, one or two triangle interceptions, the moment the army totals approach parity, and the eventual reversal. This can be edited into a short evidence montage.

## Dice Combat
5. **Low-Face Combo — 10–15s**: capture the enemy weakness/tag, the low dice in hand, then Fury → Split → Light / low-face amplification → lethal resolution. The viewer should see why a “bad” roll became the correct answer.
